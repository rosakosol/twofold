TWOFOLD · SHARE CARDS · 3a
One card, five layouts, two renditions
Every share surface in the app — game results, distance, trip and relationship stats — now draws from one card: a fixed 320×400 frame, five content layouts, three brand accents, and a dark or pastel rendition. Layout is chosen by the game, not by the user, so a Trivia result always looks like a Trivia result.
3a
Layout is decided by the game
Quote → Deep Conversation
Serif question, both answers in labelled blocks with avatars. The default for anything with two written answers.
Chat → Deep Conversation (alt)
Offered alongside Quote when both answers are short. Sender bubble takes the accent fill; partner bubble is a translucent surface.
Score → Who's More Likely To
Always a similarity percentage, so this layout is the only one those games produce. Track underneath reads the number as a proportion.
Score → This or That
Same layout, same caption. Two games sharing one result shape is the point — the category chip tells them apart.
Tally → Trivia Battle
Each player's avatar above their own score, with the question total between them. The round's own title sits above — not an instruction — and the figure is correct-out-of-total, never a similarity percentage.
Distance → the globe card
Wireframe globe instead of a black sphere, city labels on legible chips, dotted great-circle in the accent — and no category chip, because distance isn't a game.
Dark and pastel renditions
The pastel rendition is the light-mode twin: the same layout and the same accent hue, lifted to a tinted paper canvas with ink text. Both clear 4.5:1 on every label. The card follows the app theme at capture time, but nothing else about it changes.
Pastel sky · #E4F2FC → #DFF2EC, ink #16232F, accent deepened to #1F6F9E.
Pastel leaf · bubbles invert — the accent fill deepens so white text on it clears 4.5:1.
Pastel heart · replaces the old pink theme, which ran purple-on-pink and had no relationship to the palette.
Pastel distance · the globe is a graticule, so it works on a pale canvas instead of punching a black hole in it.
Pastel tally · avatar rings take a 22% ink tint, since white rings would disappear into a pale canvas.
In context
Distance, dark · Classic / Dark / Pink become the three brand accents, which is a choice about the card rather than a second theme setting.
Trivia result, dark · the action group is pinned to the safe area, dots sit under the card.
Distance, light · pastel canvas, heart accent. Swatches preview the actual card art rather than sun/moon/heart icons.
Deep Conversation, light · pastel card on the light sheet, still reading as a discrete object to be exported.
SHARE CARD SPEC
Frame
320×400 at 1×, exported at 3× (960×1200, 4:5). 28px corners, 22px padding, wordmark top-left and a footer rule carrying the date and twofold.app. Long copy reflows inside the frame; the frame never grows.
Layout by game
Deep Conversation → Quote (Chat as alternate). Who's More Likely To and This or That → Score, always a similarity percentage. Trivia Battle → Tally, correct out of total with both avatars. Distance → the globe layout.
Dark canvases
sky #123045→#08161F · leaf #0F3229→#07171B · heart #331B26→#170D14. Text #F4F9FC, accent is the light tint.
Pastel canvases
sky #E4F2FC→#DFF2EC · leaf #E3F5EA→#E7F5DE · heart #FDEAEC→#F6E6EE. Text #16232F, accent deepens to the light-mode pair, fills carry white.
Accent by category
sky = everyday questions, trivia, distance · leaf = similarity and travel · heart = the edgy set. One accent per card, on the chip, the highlight, the route line and the sender bubble. Soft labels are the primary ink at 66–72%, which still clears 4.5:1.
Globe
Drawn as a graticule — sphere outline plus four ellipses and an equator — over a 5–7% wash, so it reads on both canvases. City names sit on surface chips beside each avatar rather than as dark pills on a dark sphere.
